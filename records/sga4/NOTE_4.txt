The print is the Springer typescript of SGA 4 (Lecture Notes in Mathematics 269, 270 and 305, 1972-73); the page number in the file names is Springer's number at the foot of the page, and the typescript's own number, with the exposé numeral, stands at the top. The TeX is NOT a diplomatic transcription: it is the modern LaTeX edition of SGA 4 directed by Fabrice Orgogozo, as held in the corpus (read only). Its own form is therefore not an error: layout, line breaks, formulas set as displays or run into the text; numbering style (LaTeX numbers headings itself: the printed number is the first argument of \etiquette[...]{...} or stands in the title of a statement) and the typography of headings and labels; emphasis (the typescript's underlining set in italics or not at all, and italics the print does not have); modern spacing, guillemets for the typewriter's quotation marks, accented capitals; and macro choices for the same sign (bold for the print's double-struck letters, \Oo, \Ff, \Aa ... for the script letters, \Hhom and \Eext for the sheaf Hom and Ext, \varinjlim and \varprojlim for lim with its arrow, \cf, \ie, \resp, \etc; \quoi prints nothing; the definitions are in the edition's macros.tex, named below, if one must be looked up). Report only differences of content: words; numbers (of statements, paragraphs and formulas, and in references); formulas, indices and exponents; symbols and letters (another letter, or another alphabet where the print tells objects apart by it, as the sheaves Hom and H^q_Z from the groups Hom and H^q_Z); punctuation that changes the meaning; missing or extra text (words, lines, formulas, footnotes, arrows and labels of diagrams); wrong references. References go through labels: \hyperref[IV.subsec7.2]{7.2} prints 7.2, \eqhyperref[V.6.5.4]{6.5.4} prints (6.5.4), \hyperrefext[XII][XII.eq4.1]{4.1} prints XII 4.1, \ref{X.cor2.5} prints the number in the label's name; \cite{...} stands for the print's bibliography reference (its original label is the one in the \bibitem at the end of the exposé's file): compare the reference with the print (parentheses around it are style), not the edition's numbering of the bibliography. The edition's own devices: \sisi{A}{B} holds the print's reading A and the editors' correction B (the edition prints B): compare A with the print and report it only where it differs; B is not checked. \nde{...} is an editor's note (a footnote N.D.E.), and % begins the editors' comments: neither belongs to the print, do not report them. \pageoriginale marks the start of a printed page (it prints the page number in the margin) and stands after the page's first word or two, or after a heading or display that opens the page; its exact position is not checked. Misprints of the print, whether the TeX keeps them or corrects them (silently, as the edition does for trivial ones, or with \sisi): kind misprint_in_print, "fix": null, and say in the note whether the TeX keeps or corrects them; they are not errors of the TeX. Any other change (another word, a formula rewritten, a clause dropped or added) is a difference of content: report it.
