Convention on p526 only: the price dash, printed as an en dash like on p527 (where the TeX writes 'DM 40,--'), is typed as a single hyphen in every entry on p526. Examples: 'DM 18,-' (Vol. 178), 'DM 22,-' (Vol. 179), 'DM 16.-' (Vol. 181). Replace the ',-'/'.-' after the DM prices with ',--'/'.--' to match the print and p527.
